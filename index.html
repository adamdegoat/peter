📊 Scoring Methodology · 5 dimensions, 0–10 each
Market Demand
Size of the addressable market. How many Singapore / SE Asia businesses genuinely need this?
Buildability
How feasible is it to build solo or with a small team using current AI APIs and off-the-shelf tools?
Monetization
Revenue quality and ceiling. Is there a clear path to recurring SaaS income, one-time, or API usage fees?
Competition Gap
How weak is the existing local competition? A high gap means low-cost acquisition and pricing power.
Singapore Fit
Alignment with Singapore's regulatory environment, SME landscape, multilingual needs, and government tailwinds (e.g. MAS, IMDA grants).
